CULTURE

so, why can a nation race forward so fast without leaving its people behind?

It is a culture rooted in thousands of years of wisdom, one that prioritizes collective well-being over individual gain. Deeply influenced by Confucian values of 'benevolence' and 'harmony', Chinese culture believes that true prosperity is not measured by the height of the tallest skyscraper, but by the dignity of the most vulnerable citizen.

This cultural DNA shapes modern China. It explains why a high-speed metro still makes room for a farmer's heavy basket, and why technological progress is always paired with human empathy.

In this culture, speed is a tool for national strength, but care is the ultimate purpose.
